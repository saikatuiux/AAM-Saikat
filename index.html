A proven process for repeatable product success.

  1. 01

    Discovery, User Research & Audits

    Conducting stakeholder interviews, user journey mapping, competitive benchmarking, and heuristic friction audits to align product requirements with core business OKRs and customer pain points.

  2. 02

    Information Architecture & Wireframing

Recruiter & Client FAQ

Common questions & working models.

For 0-to-1 initiatives, I focus on rapid discovery, user interviews, low-fidelity wireframes, and fast prototype validation to prove product-market fit before scaling. For legacy enterprise software, I perform detailed heuristic audits, identify high-friction bottlenecks, and implement modular design systems that modernize usability without alienating existing power users.

Design must drive measurable business outcomes. I measure success through concrete product metrics: onboarding completion rates, task execution velocity, churn reduction, feature adoption, and Net Promoter Score (NPS). For design system projects, ROI is measured in accelerated engineering velocity and reduced bug remediation hours.

I treat cross-functional collaboration as fundamental. By engaging engineers during early discovery, documenting comprehensive component edge cases (empty states, errors, latency, focus states), and using structured Figma design tokens, we eliminate handoff friction and build shared product ownership.

I am available for Lead UI/UX Designer full-time positions, Fractional Design Leadership (10–20 hours/week) for venture-backed startups, and targeted 4–8 week Design Sprints focused on product discovery, redesigns, or design systems.
